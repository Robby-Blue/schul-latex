\section{The Story of an Hour}
\emph{The Story of an Hour}, written by \emph{Kate Chopin}, released in 1894 is a short story dealing with the role of marriage in a womans life in the 19th century.
 
The story follows Louise Mallard, her husband, Brentley Mallard, and her sister, Josephine.
 
\subsection{Plot}
Louise is known to have a weak heart. When the news break that her husband has died in a train accident, Josephine tries to to tell her as calmly as possible.
 
Louise is terribly sad and shocked. She sits in her room, motionless, just listening to and watching the outside, sobbing. Suddenly she feels something, something relaxed and warm. She exclaims \emph{free, free, free!} because she realised that she will be independent from now on.
 
Josephine, waiting outside, is worried and asks her to come out. Louise does, appearing triumphantly.
 
Brentley Mallard comes in; he didn't actually die.
 
Louise is shocked, has a heart attack and dies.
