\section{The First Feminists}
\emph{The First Feminists}, written by Bernandine Evaristo and published in 2020, is a modern short story about humanity's history.
 
\subsection{Plot}
An unnamed narrator presents herself as the speaker of the Founding Mothers of the Human Gene Pool.
 
The Founding Mothers have been there since before humans evolved, since the extinction of the dinosaurs. They witnessed the creation of the world; the continentral drit, the ice age, etc.
 
They were the first humans on the planet, the first tribes, the first pioneers.
 
Their intelligence and brains developed so they could invent fire. They become stronger, could walk upright, migrated all over the world.
 
Concepts of femininity and masculinity didn't exist, everyone was equal.
 
Men developed more muscle on average, and wanted more power, demanding women to stay at home. Love didn't matter.
 
The Founding Mothers survived catastrophes, but worry about humanity's future, fearing a global catastrophe.
 
They're nostalgic about the past, before inventions and concepts.
