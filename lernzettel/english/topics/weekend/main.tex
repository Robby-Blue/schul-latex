\section{Weekend}
The short story \emph{Weekend}, written by \emph{Fay Weldon} and released in 1978, probably playing at roughly the same time, highlights the amount of unpaid labour many women have to perform, even during what is usually leisure time.
 
\subsection{Summary}
The short story follows Martha and her family (her husband Martin and their three children) on their weekend trip (Friday-Sunday) to their cottage in the country. Their friends are there too. 
 
Throughout the whole story Martha does most of the work, from preparing food and games, making food for everyone, making everyones beds, taking care of their children, cleaning everything up, etc.
 
Martin does basically nothing but patronise and criticize her.
